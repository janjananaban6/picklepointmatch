How ranking is calculated

Most wins rank first. Ties are broken by higher win rate (wins ÷ recorded matches), then fewer losses, more recorded matches, and alphabetical order. Only players with at least one valid recorded result are ranked; unfinished matches do not count. The displayed win rate is rounded, but ranking uses the exact win-rate ratio.
